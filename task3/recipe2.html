HOW TO COOK CHAPATI

SERVES:4|COOK:50 MINUTES|TOTAL:45-50 MINIUTES

INGREDIENTS

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil (or melted butter)
  • 1 cup warm water (add gradually)
  • Extra flour for dusting
  • Cooking oil for frying

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. In a large bowl, mix the flour and salt.
  2. Add the cooking oil and rub it into the flour.
  3. Gradually add the warm water while mixing until a soft dough forms.
  4. Knead the dough for 8-10 minutes until it becomes soft and elastic.
  5. Cover the dough and let it rest for 20-30 minutes.
  6. Divide the dough into 6 equal balls.
  7. Roll each ball into a thin,round circle on a lightly floured surface.
  8. Heat a frying pan or skillet over medium heat.
  9. Place one chapati on the hot pan and cook until golden brown.
  10. Flip it over,brush with little oil and cook until golden brown.
  11. Repeat with the remaining dough

    12. Serve with any vegies or beef stew or any drink 🍽
