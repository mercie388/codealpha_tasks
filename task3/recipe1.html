HOW TO COOK BEEF PILAU

SERVES:4|COOK:50 MINUTES|TOTAL:1 HOUR 10 MINIUTES

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 cups of rice
  • 500g beef, cut into cubes
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minched
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 tsp pilau masala
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 4 cups water
  • Salt to taste

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Heat the cooking oil in a large pot.
  2. Fry the chopped onion until golden brown.
  3. Add the garlic and ginger, then cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the beef and cook until it is browned.
  5. Stir in the pilau masala and salt.
  6. Add the rice and mix well with the beef and spices.
  7. Pour in the water and bring it to a boil.
  8. Reduce the heat, cover the pot, and cook until the rice is tender and the water has been absorbed.
  9. Fluff the pilau with a fork and serve hot.

    10. Serve with kachumbari 🍽
